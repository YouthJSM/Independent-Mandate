Voting outside your district needs advance notice.

    1. Arrive with valid ID

      Bring your original Smart ID card, green ID book or valid temporary ID certificate.

    2. Verify your identity

      An official checks your ID against the voters' roll and directs you to a voting tablet.

    3. Choose your option on the tablet

      Tap the option you want. You can change your choice before you confirm.

    4. Confirm your vote

      Review your choice and tap Confirm. Your vote is cast in secret.

    5. See your confirmation

      The tablet shows a confirmation screen. You are done. Nothing is printed and there are no paper ballots.
